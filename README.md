
# Hi! 

### PhD Researcher in Computer Science | University College Dublin 🇮🇪

I am a PhD researcher at **University College Dublin (UCD)**, working on **AI-based weather forecasting** using meteorological reanalysis data.

My research focuses on developing accurate, interpretable, and reliable machine learning models for weather prediction, with a particular interest in **precipitation forecasting over Ireland**.

### 🔬 Research Interests

- AI for Weather and Climate
- Spatiotemporal Machine Learning and Graph Neural Networks
- Probabilistic Forecasting and Uncertainty Quantification
- Physics-Guided and Explainable AI
- Extreme Weather Prediction and Forecast Reliability
- ERA5 Reanalysis and Meteorological Data Analysis

### 🌦️ Selected Research Projects

**Interpretable Deep Learning for Precipitation Forecasting**

Developing and evaluating deep learning architectures for short-term precipitation prediction using ERA5, with a focus on model interpretability and probabilistic reliability.

[View Repository](https://github.com/Liilychee/Interpretable-BU-LSTM-for-Rainfall-Prediction-in-Dublin)

**Physics-Guided Probabilistic Precipitation Forecasting**

Investigating physics-guided quantile models and cross-regional transfer learning for precipitation forecasting from the UK to Ireland.

[View Repository](https://github.com/Liilychee/physics-guided-Quantile-Probabilistic-Precipitation)

**Graph-Based Spatiotemporal Forecasting**

Investigating geographical and wind-directed graph representations for precipitation forecasting, with an emphasis on regional variability and model robustness.

**Current Research**

- Attribution-regularised residual probabilistic forecasting
- Diagnosing failure modes of AI weather forecasts under high-impact weather conditions
